About us

Warm care for your personal space

espresso clean began with a simple idea: your home should be a sanctuary, not another thing on the to-do list.

Trust, detail and a little calm

Hi, we're Anna and Roman. Letting someone into your home takes trust, so we keep things small and personal. Both of us are police-checked, and we focus on long-term relationships, clear communication and no surprises.

We clean houses and apartments across the Mornington Peninsula and south east Melbourne.

The name says it all. Think of the first cup of the day: warm, comforting and grounding. That's the feeling we want you to walk into when you come home.
