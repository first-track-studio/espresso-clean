FAQs

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first clean.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients give us a key or lockbox code, or let us in and head out. We'll agree on the best arrangement with you.

Are your cleaners police-checked?

Yes. All of our cleaners are police-checked, so you can feel comfortable having them in your home.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring our own eco-minded cleaning products, cloths and vacuum. If you prefer a particular product for a delicate surface, just leave it out for us.

What about pets?

We love pets. Let us know about any furry family members when you book and we'll take care to keep them safe and comfortable.

How is the price worked out?

It depends on the size of your home, the type of clean and how often you'd like us. Send us a quote request and we'll reply with a clear price.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Life happens. We just ask for as much notice as you can give so we can offer your spot to someone else.

Are there lock-in contracts?

No. Regular cleans are flexible, and you can adjust or pause as your needs change.
